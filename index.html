Web app · Qloo Agentic Hackathon 2026

TasteAbroad

Live demo Code Devpost

Students pick a city to study abroad from rankings and tuition, then find out the city doesn't fit them. TasteAbroad asks a different question: will I feel at home there? You type what you love, an AI agent (Gemini with function calling) asks Qloo's taste API to rank 105 university cities once for each of your tastes, and you get your top matches with a score you can check: every card shows where the city ranks for each taste. Universities, tuition, living costs, Erasmus+ and scholarship links come with it.

  • Scores come only from Qloo's data; the AI picks the lookups and writes the notes.
  • Searches show cities in about 10 to 20 seconds, even when the free Gemini tier is slow: two models are asked at once and the first answer wins.
  • Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Qloo API, Gemini API, deployed on Vercel.
